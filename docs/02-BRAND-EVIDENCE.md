  * *Origin / Evidence:* Iconic color of the upper and lower triangular arrows in the brand mark (`imgi_1.jpg`) and illuminated orange acrylic elements in the physical store wall neon (`imgi_5.jpg`, `imgi_14.jpg`, `imgi_15.jpg`), as well as brand text in poster (`imgi_18.jpg`).

  * *Origin / Evidence:* Main display numerals `"144"` and wordmark `"Burger"` in the logo (`imgi_1.jpg`) and glass neon tube illumination (`imgi_14.jpg`).

## 2. TYPOGRAPHY PERSONALITY EVIDENCE

### 2.1 Display Numerals ("144")
* **Personality & Structure:** Heavy, geometric, bold sans-serif with high vertical authority and sharp squared terminals.
* **Evidence:** The core "144" anchor in `imgi_1.jpg`, `imgi_14.jpg`, and `imgi_18.jpg`.

### 2.2 Wordmark ("Burger")
* **Personality & Structure:** Friendly, custom semi-cursive neon script with rounded connections and warm, unpretentious curves (distinctive 'B', flowing 'u', 'r', 'g', 'e', 'r').
* **Evidence:** Physical neon sign `imgi_14.jpg` and logo mark `imgi_1.jpg`.

### 2.3 Brand Tagline & Motto Typography ("FEITO PRA MARCAR")
* **Personality & Structure:** Distressed brush / urban chalk typography conveying craft authenticity, energy, and urban grit.
* **Evidence:** Founder poster artwork `imgi_18.jpg` displaying *"144 BURGER FEITO PRA MARCAR"*, *"SABOR ATITUDE PROPÓSITO"*, and chalkboard values.

---

## 3. GRAPHIC MOTIFS & ICONOGRAPHY

* **Dual Directional Triangles:** An upward-pointing triangle arrow at top and a downward-pointing triangle arrow at bottom. Functionally symbolizes a burger composition (top bun and bottom bun arrows pointing inward) while creating an unmistakable brand stamp.
* **Circular Arc Frame:** A semicircular perimeter arc enclosing the central numerals.
* **Double Accent Underline:** Two parallel horizontal bars anchoring the script wordmark.
* **Neon Tube Glow:** Subtle halos, rounded stroke caps, and illuminated wireframe aesthetic reflecting the physical store's atmosphere.

---

## 4. TEXTURES & ENVIRONMENTAL ATMOSPHERE

* **Dark Natural Wood:** Deep walnut and oak cutting boards and dining tables (`imgi_15.jpg`, `imgi_19.jpg`).
* **Matte Interior Plaster with Neon Halos:** Smooth dark/warm walls illuminated by ambient glowing neon tubes (`imgi_14.jpg`, `imgi_15.jpg`).
* **Viscous Culinary Textures:** High-gloss brioche sheen, melting cheese, slow-cooked shredded beef rib fibers.

---

## 5. PHOTOGRAPHY PERSONALITY & LIGHTING

* **Perspective:** Extreme macro close-ups (3:4 portrait) creating immense appetite appeal.
* **Lighting Style:** Warm, directional spotlighting with soft ambient falloff against dark backgrounds, highlighting fresh glistening ingredients without artificial studio detachment.
* **Atmosphere:** Urban, craft-centric, passionate, intimate.

---

## 6. PRELIMINARY BRAND EVIDENCE SUMMARY

* **Brand Atmosphere:** Urban artisanal craft burger joint combining intense culinary appetite appeal with warm neighborhood hospitality.
* **Color Direction:** Dark charcoal canvas illuminated by energetic neon orange (`#E55A1B`) and neon yellow (`#F5B800`) accents, supported by natural brioche, beef rib, and rustic wood tones.
* **Typography Pairing Potential:** Heavy bold geometric sans (for display impact) paired with warm humanist/clean sans for UI copy and expressive script/brush accents for brand personality.
